Quick answers

The short version of what most visitors come here for.

How many characters is this?

Paste the text into the character counter and the answer appears as you type, with spaces counted and without them.

What is my word count?

The same page works as a word count checker. Words, sentences, paragraphs and reading time all update live.

How many words is one page?

About 250 words on a double spaced page and about 500 single spaced. The words to pages calculator converts any word count for your font and spacing.
