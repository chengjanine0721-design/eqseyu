Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Overview

eqseyu.com ("we", "our", "the site") provides free browser based tools. This policy explains what data we collect and how we handle it. The short version: your tool input never leaves your browser.

2. Data you enter into our tools

All tools on this site (including the Character Counter) process text entirely on your device using client side JavaScript. We do not receive, transmit, store or log any text you type or paste. Closing the tab erases it.

3. Information collected automatically

Like nearly all websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process standard technical logs for security and performance purposes, including IP addresses, browser type and pages visited. These logs are governed by Cloudflare's privacy policy and are not used by us for advertising or profiling.

4. Cookies and analytics

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels or third party analytics. If we ever add privacy respecting aggregate analytics, this policy will be updated first.

5. Children's privacy

The site is safe for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Your rights

Since we do not collect or store personal data beyond the technical logs described above, there is generally nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. If you have questions or believe your rights under GDPR, CCPA or other applicable laws have been affected, contact us via the Contact page.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Contact page.
