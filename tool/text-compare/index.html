Text Compare

Spot the difference between two pieces of writing with a text compare online tool that highlights changed words and shows a similarity score.

Differences (removed = strike, added = highlight)

What is a text compare online tool?

A text compare online tool places two pieces of writing side by side and shows what changed. This free difference checker online highlights removed and added words and gives a similarity percentage, so you can spot edits instantly.

Compare two texts word by word

Paste your original into Text A and the revised version into Text B. The tool works as a paragraph comparison tool, matching words in order and marking the first point where the texts diverge. A similarity checker between two texts score appears above the result.

A handy text matcher

Whether you are proofreading an essay, checking a contract edit or tracking changes to a blog post, this text matcher makes the differences obvious. Everything runs locally, so your drafts stay private.

Frequently asked questions

How does a text compare online tool work?
It splits both texts into words, finds the shared words in order, then highlights where they differ. A similarity score summarises how much overlap there is.
Can I use this as a difference checker online?
Yes. Removed words appear struck through and added words are highlighted, so the difference checker online view makes edits easy to spot.
What does the similarity percentage mean?
It is the share of shared words between the two texts. A higher similarity checker between two texts score means the texts are closer.
Is this a paragraph comparison tool?
It compares word by word across whatever you paste, which works for a single sentence, a paragraph or several paragraphs.
Can I use it as a text matcher for drafts?
Yes. Paste two versions of a draft to see matched and changed text. The text matcher runs in your browser, so your writing stays private.
