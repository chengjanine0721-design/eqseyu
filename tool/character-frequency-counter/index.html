Character Frequency Counter

See how often each letter appears with a character frequency counter that ranks your characters. A simple letter frequency counter for text analysis and cryptograms.

Character frequency (sorted)

What is a character frequency counter?

A character frequency counter shows how many times each character appears in a piece of text. This free letter frequency counter sorts results from most to least common, so you can see the distribution of letters at a glance.

How to count letter frequency

Paste your text, choose whether to count letters only and whether to ignore case, then the tool will count letter frequency for you. Each character is shown with its count and percentage of the total, ranked by use.

Why letter frequency matters

The letter frequency of English text follows a well-known pattern, with “e”, “t” and “a” at the top. Comparing your text to that pattern is useful for linguistics, cryptogram solving, and checking whether a sample looks natural. This frequency letter counter makes the comparison instant.

Frequently asked questions

What does a character frequency counter do?
It tallies how often each character appears in your text and sorts the results, showing both a count and a percentage for each one.
Can I use it as a letter frequency counter?
Yes. Choose “Letters only” and the letter frequency counter will ignore spaces, numbers and punctuation, counting just A to Z.
Does it ignore capital letters?
By default it ignores case so “A” and “a” are combined. Switch to case sensitive to see them separately.
What is letter frequency used for?
Letter frequency analysis is used in cryptography, linguistics, text licensing checks and language learning to understand how text is distributed.
Can I count letter frequency in long text?
You can paste text of any length. The frequency letter counter updates instantly, even for long passages.
