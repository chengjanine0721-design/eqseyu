Backwards Text Generator

Turn any text upside down and back to front with a backwards text generator that runs instantly in your browser. Flip characters, reverse words, or spell everything in reverse.

Reversed output

What is a backwards text generator?

A backwards text generator flips the order of the letters or words in any passage. It is the simplest kind of text reverser: paste your text, pick a mode, and get instant backwards text you can copy anywhere. Everything happens in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

Three ways to reverse text

Our reverse text tool offers three modes. Flip all characters mirrors the entire string, so “hello” becomes “olleh”. Reverse word order keeps each word intact but reads the sentence from the end, a handy word reverser for puzzles. Reverse each word flips every word individually while keeping them in place.

People also call this a backwards translator, because the result looks like a different language even though every original character is still there.

Where backwards text is useful

Use reversed text for social media captions, escape-room clues, classroom icebreakers, secret notes and design mockups. Because this is an online text reverser, you can also paste the output straight back in to return to the original text with a single flip.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make backwards text?
Paste your text above, choose one of the three modes and click Generate. The backwards text generator flips it instantly and the Copy button puts the result on your clipboard.
Does this reverse text or just words?
Both. Flip all characters reverses every letter; Reverse word order reverses the sequence of words; Reverse each word flips letters inside each word. Pick whichever reverse text mode you need.
Is there a word reverser for whole sentences?
Yes. The Reverse word order mode acts as a word reverser, keeping spelling intact while reading the sentence backwards from the last word to the first.
Can a backwards translator handle punctuation?
It preserves punctuation and spacing exactly where they are in character-flip mode, so symbols stay in their mirrored positions. This makes the backwards translator safe for posts and messages.
Is my text stored by this text reverser?
No. The text reverser runs entirely with JavaScript on your device. Your text is never sent to a server, and closing the tab erases it.
