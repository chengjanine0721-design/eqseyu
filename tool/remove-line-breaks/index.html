Remove Line Breaks

Clean up pasted text with a tool that lets you remove line breaks in one click. Replace newlines with a space, comma or nothing at all using this line break remover.

Clean text

Why remove line breaks?

Text copied from PDFs, emails or documents often arrives with stray line breaks in the middle of sentences. A line break remover joins those fragments back into clean, continuous text so you can paste it into a form, message or editor without odd gaps.

Choose how to replace newlines

This new line remover lets you delete line breaks entirely or replace each one with a space, comma or pipe. If you need to remove line feed characters and collapse empty lines at the same time, this tool trims and rejoins automatically.

A fast linebreak remover

Because it runs in your browser, this linebreak remover works instantly on long text. To remove newlines, just paste, pick a separator and copy the cleaned result. No upload, no signup.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove line breaks from text?
Paste your text above, choose what each break should become (a space, comma or nothing) and click Remove line breaks. The cleaned text appears in the output box.
Can I delete line breaks without adding spaces?
Yes. Choose “Nothing” as the replacement and the tool will delete line breaks, joining the lines directly together.
Will this remove line feed and empty lines?
It does. The tool splits on both Windows and Mac line endings, trims each line and drops empty ones, so stray line feeds and blank lines disappear.
What is a line break remover good for?
A line break remover is useful for cleaning text copied from PDFs, spreadsheets or chat, where hard wraps break sentences across multiple lines.
Does this new line remover store my text?
No. The new line remover runs entirely on your device with JavaScript, so your text never leaves the browser.
