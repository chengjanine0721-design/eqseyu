Hashtag Counter

Count hashtags in posts and captions with a hashtag counter that lists every tag. Also see how word count treats hashtags so you can write within limits.

Hashtags found

What is a hashtag counter?

A hashtag counter finds and counts every #tag in your text. This free tool shows the total number of hashtags, the number of unique tags, and lists each one, so you can manage tags on social posts and captions.

Does a hashtag count as a word in word count?

A common question is: does a hashtag count as a word in word count? Most counters treat a hashtag as a single word, and in word count hashtag counts as one word including the # symbol. Our tool shows your words with tags excluded, so you can account for them either way.

Why count hashtags?

Social platforms reward relevant, moderate hashtag use. Running a hashtag counter helps you avoid duplicate tags, keep captions tidy and stay within character limits while still reaching the right audience.

Frequently asked questions

What does a hashtag counter do?
It scans your text for words that start with #, counts them and shows both the total and the number of unique hashtags.
Does a hashtag count as a word in word count?
Typically yes. In most tools word count hashtag counts as one word, treating the #tag as a single token. This counter also shows your word total with hashtags removed.
How do I find unique hashtags?
The hashtag counter removes duplicates (ignoring case) and shows the unique hashtags separately from the total count.
Can I count hashtags in a long caption?
Yes. Paste any caption or post into the counter and it will list and count all hashtags instantly.
Is the hashtag counter free and private?
It is free and runs entirely in your browser, so your captions are never uploaded.
