Facebook Text Formatter

Make your posts stand out with Facebook text formatting that actually works. Turn plain words into bold, italic or script styles and paste them straight into Facebook.

Formatted text

What is a Facebook text formatter?

A facebook text formatter converts ordinary letters into stylised unicode characters such as bold, italic and script. Because Facebook does not offer a bold button, this facebook text formatting trick uses unicode symbols that display in posts, comments and bios.

How to format facebook text

Type your facebook text, pick a style and copy the result. This facebook post formatter supports bold, italic, bold italic, script, double-struck and monospace, so you can match the look of your post.

Tips for facebook formatting

Use fb text formatting sparingly for headings and emphasis rather than whole paragraphs, since stylised letters can be harder to read. Not every device renders every unicode style, so preview your facebook formatting before publishing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make bold text on Facebook?
Type your text above, choose the Bold style and copy the result. The facebook text formatter converts letters to unicode bold characters you can paste into any post.
Does Facebook text formatting work everywhere?
Most modern devices and browsers display these unicode styles, but some older phones may show a fallback font. Preview your facebook text formatting before posting.
Can I format a whole Facebook post?
You can, but for readability it is best to use facebook formatting on short phrases, names or headings rather than long paragraphs.
Is this a free facebook post formatter?
Yes. This facebook post formatter is free, runs in your browser and needs no login.
Will the styled text count differently?
The styled characters are still text, so they copy and paste normally. This fb text formatting does not change spelling, only the glyphs shown.
