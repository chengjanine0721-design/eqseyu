How to hit your word count (without padding)

  • Under the target? Add a concrete example to each body paragraph, then explicitly explain what the example proves. Evidence plus analysis is the fastest legitimate way to add 100 to 150 words.
  • Add a counter argument. One paragraph acknowledging an opposing view and rebutting it typically adds 120 to 200 words and usually improves the grade.
  • Expand your conclusion. Don't just restate, connect your argument to a wider implication, limitation or next question.
  • Over the target? Cut filler openers ("It is important to note that…"), redundant adverbs, and any example that doesn't support your thesis.
  • Never pad with repetition. Graders can tell, and it costs marks.

What counts toward the word limit?

ElementUsually counted?
Body textYes
Title and headingsSometimes, check your rubric
In text citations (Smith, 2020)Sometimes, APA counts them, MLA generally excludes them from the page limit
Reference list / bibliographyAlmost never
FootnotesUsually excluded

Heads up: this counter counts everything you paste, including headings and citations. If your rubric excludes them, cut them out before measuring, or aim for the lower end of the range.

Frequently asked questions

How many words should a 1,000 word essay have in each section?
A balanced split: introduction ~100 words, four body paragraphs of ~180 words each, and a conclusion ~120 words. That leaves a little headroom for closing sentences.
How many pages is a 1,000 word essay?
About 4 pages double spaced in 12 pt Times New Roman, or 2 pages single spaced. Use our words to pages calculator for other fonts and spacing.
Does the counter include the title?
Yes, every word you paste is counted. Leave the title out of the box if your rubric excludes it.
Is there a length limit?
No. The counter handles essays from 50 to 20,000+ words instantly.
Is my essay uploaded anywhere?
No. All counting happens locally in your browser, nothing is transmitted or stored.
