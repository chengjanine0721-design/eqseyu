Comma Counter

Count commas in a paragraph or essay with a comma counter that shows total commas and punctuation density. A quick commas calculator for writers and editors.

What is a comma counter?

A comma counter tallies how many commas appear in your text. This free commas calculator also reports commas per sentence and as a share of your words, giving you a quick read on punctuation density.

How to count commas in your writing

Paste any passage and the comma count updates instantly. It is useful for checking list-heavy sentences, comparing editorial styles, or teaching punctuation. To count commas across a long document, just paste the whole thing.

Why comma count matters

Too few commas make sentences hard to parse; too many break the flow. Watching your comma count alongside sentence length helps you find a natural rhythm. Everything happens in the browser, so this comma counter keeps your text private.

Frequently asked questions

What does a comma counter do?
It counts every comma in your text and shows extra stats like commas per sentence and commas as a percentage of words.
How do I count commas in a paragraph?
Paste the paragraph above and the comma counter updates automatically, showing the total comma count as you type.
Can this be used as a commas calculator?
Yes. The commas calculator reports total commas, commas per sentence and per word so you can analyse punctuation density.
Does it count commas inside numbers?
It counts every comma character, including thousands separators like 1,000, so use plain numbers if you only want punctuation commas.
Is the comma counter free?
Yes. It is free, needs no signup and runs entirely in your browser.
