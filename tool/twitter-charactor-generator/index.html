How X counts characters

Twitter doesn't count characters one for one. Its counting rules (twitter text) assign a weight to each character based on its Unicode range:

  • Latin letters, digits, standard punctuation and spaces → 1
  • CJK (Chinese, Japanese, Korean), Cyrillic, Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari and most other scripts → 2
  • Most emoji → 2 (emoji with skin tone modifiers or ZWJ sequences can be higher)
  • Any URL → 23, no matter how long it really is

That's why a 140 character Chinese post and a 280 character English post both fill a single tweet.

When your post is too long

  1. Trim ruthlessly first. Cut adverbs, hedge words ("just", "really", "I think"), and any sentence that restates the previous one.
  2. Turn the link into a shorter phrase. Links cost a flat 23 characters, so shortening the URL text doesn't help, replacing it with "link in bio" might.
  3. Split into a thread. Use the thread splitter on this page: it breaks your text at word boundaries so each part fits within the limit.
  4. Move detail to a reply. Post the hook, then reply with the examples, threads get more reach than one dense post anyway.
Thread tip: number your parts (1/5, 2/5 …) before posting. Readers drop off unnumbered threads because they can't tell how much is left.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my counter show 300 when my editor says 280?
Your editor likely counts every character as 1. This tool applies Twitter's weighted counting, so CJK characters and emoji are counted as 2 and links as 23, matching what X actually enforces.
Do emoji count as one or two characters?
Usually two. Simple emoji are two code units. Emoji with skin tone modifiers, gender signs or ZWJ sequences (like family emoji) can count as more.
Does a link really count as 23 characters?
Yes. Every URL is shortened internally (t.co) and counted as 23 characters, regardless of its real length.
Can I write longer posts?
With X Premium you can publish long posts up to 25,000 characters. Switch the limit selector above to 25,000 to check against that instead.
Is my draft uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything is calculated in your browser, nothing is sent to a server or stored.

Why the numbers differ from other counters.

How many characters is this post?

Why do other counters disagree with X?

Most tools count every character as one. X shortens links and weights some scripts, which this page reproduces.

Yes. The thread splitter breaks your text at word boundaries and numbers every part.
