Syllable Counter

Count syllables in words, sentences and whole paragraphs with a syllable counter that also works as a haiku checker. See the 5-7-5 haiku syllable count as you type.

Per-word breakdown

What is a syllable counter?

A syllable counter splits words into their beats, or syllables. This free online syllable counter estimates the syllable count of any word, sentence or paragraph using English vowel patterns, so you can check pronunciation, reading level or poetic meter without counting on your fingers.

Count syllables in any text

Type or paste your text and the syllable checker shows a per-word breakdown plus the total. It handles common English rules such as silent “e” and vowel pairs, which is why it is a reliable way to count syllables for school assignments, ESL practice and lyric writing.

Built-in haiku checker

Separate three lines with a slash ( / ) or a line break and the tool becomes a haiku checker. It reports the haiku syllable count of each line against the classic 5-7-5 pattern, so this doubles as a dedicated haiku syllable counter for poets and students.

Frequently asked questions

How does a syllable counter work?
It looks at groups of vowels in each word and applies common English rules, such as dropping a silent final “e”. The syllable counter then adds the beats across all words to give you a total.
Can this check my haiku syllable count?
Yes. Enter three lines separated by a slash or a line break and the haiku checker shows each line's syllable count and tells you whether it matches the 5-7-5 haiku syllable count.
Is the syllable counter accurate?
English spelling is irregular, so any online syllable counter is an estimate. This tool follows standard vowel rules and is accurate for the vast majority of common words.
Can I use it as a syllable checker for long text?
You can paste a full paragraph into the syllable checker. It will list each word with its count and show the average syllables per word across the whole passage.
Does it count syllables in haiku only?
No. The haiku syllable counter mode is optional. Without slashes or line breaks, the tool simply counts syllables in whatever text you paste.
