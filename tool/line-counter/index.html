Line Counter

Get an exact line count for any text with a line counter that reports total lines, non-empty lines and blank lines. A simple way to count lines online.

What is a line counter?

A line counter counts how many lines are in a block of text. This free linecounter also reports non-empty lines, blank lines, word count and the length of the longest line, giving you a complete line count at a glance.

Count lines in any text

Paste your content and the tool will count lines instantly, including empty ones if you want them. Writers use it to check script formatting, developers use it to count line entries in a list, and teachers use it to measure answer lengths.

Line count plus word count

Need a line count word breakdown too? This tool shows both, so you can see how many lines and words your text has without switching between utilities. Everything updates live as you type.

Frequently asked questions

How does a line counter work?
It splits your text at every line break and counts the resulting lines, then separates empty lines from lines that contain characters.
Does it count blank lines?
Yes. The line counter shows total lines plus non-empty and blank lines separately, so you can include or ignore empty lines as needed.
Can I count lines and words at once?
You can. This tool shows the line count and word count together, which is handy when you need a line count word summary for an assignment.
Is there a limit to how many lines I can count?
No practical limit. The line counter works on pasted text of any size, from a few lines to thousands.
Is my text uploaded when I count lines?
No. The linecounter runs entirely in your browser, so nothing is uploaded or stored.
