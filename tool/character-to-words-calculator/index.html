How characters and words relate

There's no fixed conversion, it depends on the words you use. But English text is remarkably consistent on average: about 5 characters per word including the space (roughly 4.7 letters plus a space). That's why a 1,000 character limit usually means around 190 to 200 words.

How to get an exact count

Frequently asked questions

How many words is 1,000 characters?
About 196 words on average (5.1 characters per word including spaces). Real text usually lands between 171 and 220 words.
How many characters are in a 500 word essay?
Roughly 2,550 characters with spaces, or about 2,350 without spaces, assuming typical English word length.
Do characters include spaces?
Most platforms count spaces. Our default average of 5.1 includes the space after each word. The "with spaces removed" column shows the visible character figure.
Can I convert words to characters too?
Yes, switch to the Words → Characters tab and enter your word count.
Is my text sent anywhere?
No. All counting happens in your browser with JavaScript.
