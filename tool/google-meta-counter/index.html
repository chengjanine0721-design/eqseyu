How Google measures title and description length

Google does not count characters first, it measures pixels. The same 60 characters can render narrower or wider depending on the letters, so a title full of wide capitals hits the limit sooner than one with narrow letters like i, l and t. That is why this counter shows both numbers, character count for editing and pixel width for the real limit.

The measurement here uses the Arial font metrics Google results are rendered with, on a desktop layout of about 600 pixels for titles and 990 pixels for descriptions. Mobile results are narrower in practice but render titles slightly larger, so the safe character ranges shown in the table above already account for it.

Writing titles that fit

  • Front load the keyword. The words at the start of a title carry the most weight and survive truncation.
  • Put the brand last. If the title gets cut, it is usually the tail that disappears, so keep your site name there.
  • Count every word. Wide letters such as W and M take almost the space of two narrow ones, check the pixel number rather than trusting the character count alone.
  • One idea per title. Separate two topics with a pipe or a dash only when both matter to searchers.

Writing descriptions that fit

  • Aim for 150 to 160 characters. Long enough to make the case, short enough to avoid the cut.
  • Front load the promise. Say what the page gives the reader in the first 80 characters.
  • Include a call to action. Try it, learn how, or check yours today, near the end while space allows.
  • Do not stuff keywords. Google often rewrites stuffed descriptions, so plain natural sentences survive longest.

Frequently asked questions

How many characters is this title in pixels?
Characters and pixels measure different things. Roughly 9 to 11 average characters fit in 100 pixels of Google title text, but wide capitals can take double the width of narrow letters. The meter above measures the exact pixel width of what you type.
What is the ideal meta description length?
Between 150 and 160 characters for desktop results. Google truncates around 990 pixels, which is usually close to 155 characters of typical English text.
Will Google rewrite my title anyway?
Sometimes. Google rewrites titles it considers too long, too short, stuffed with keywords, or a poor match for the content. Staying inside the pixel limit gives your own wording the best chance of being shown.
Does the description pixel count include spaces?
Yes, spaces and punctuation render real width, and both are included in the measurement just as they are in the search results.
Is my text uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything runs locally in your browser, the preview and counters never transmit your text.

Meta tag questions

Quick answers while you optimize.

How do I check my word count online?

Type in the box above and the character, pixel and word counts update as you type, no button to press.

Why is my title cut off in Google?

The pixel width passed the limit. Shorten the tail or move the brand name forward until the meter turns green.

Do keywords in the description help ranking?

Not directly, but Google bolds the words searchers typed, so a matching description lifts click through rate.
