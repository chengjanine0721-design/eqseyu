Sentence Counter

Find out how many sentences are in your writing with a sentence counter that also reports word count and average sentence length. A quick online sentence counter for students and writers.

Sentence list

What is a sentence counter?

A sentence counter tells you how many sentences are in a piece of writing. This online sentence counter splits your text on sentence-ending punctuation and shows a numbered sentence list alongside word count and average sentence length.

How many sentences are in my text?

Wondering how many sentences you have written? Paste your paragraph and the sentences counter answers instantly. It is useful for checking assignment length, balancing paragraph structure and editing for readability.

Count sentences for better writing

The sentence count and words-per-sentence figure help you spot run-ons and choppy writing. A good target for clear prose is roughly 15 to 20 words per sentence, and this tool makes it easy to count sentences as you revise.

Frequently asked questions

How does a sentence counter detect sentences?
It splits text at periods, exclamation marks and question marks followed by a space and a capital letter, then lists each sentence with its number.
Can I use this as an online sentence counter for essays?
Yes. Paste any essay or paragraph into the online sentence counter to see the sentence count, total words and average sentence length.
Does it handle abbreviations?
It uses common punctuation rules, so abbreviations with periods may be treated as sentence endings. You can still read the numbered list and adjust manually. If you searched for a "sentance counter", this is the same tool.
What does the sentence count tell me?
The sentence count shows how many sentences your text contains, which helps you judge paragraph length and writing rhythm at a glance.
Is there a sentences counter for long documents?
This sentences counter works on pasted text of any length, so you can check a single paragraph or a full page of writing.
