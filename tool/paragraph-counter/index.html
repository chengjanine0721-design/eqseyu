Paragraph Counter

Check how many paragraphs your text has with a paragraph counter that also shows words per paragraph. See how many words are in a paragraph at a glance.

Words per paragraph

What is a paragraph counter?

A paragraph counter tells you how many paragraphs are in your text and breaks down the length of each one. This free paragraph count tool also shows total words and average words per paragraph, so you can judge structure at a glance.

How many paragraphs are in my text?

Wondering how many paragraphs you have written? Paste your draft and the tool counts each block of text separated by a blank line. If you are unsure whether your spacing is this a paragraph or just a line break, the per-paragraph word list makes it clear.

Check how many words are in a paragraph

To count paragraphs and see their length, look at the words-per-paragraph list below the stats. It answers how many words are in a paragraph for every block, which helps you keep paragraphs balanced and readable.

Frequently asked questions

How do I count paragraphs in my text?
Paste your text and separate paragraphs with a blank line. The paragraph counter counts each block and shows its word count.
How many words are in a paragraph?
The tool lists each paragraph with its exact word count, so you can see how many words are in a paragraph without counting manually.
What counts as a paragraph?
A paragraph is a block of text followed by a blank line. If you are unsure if this is a paragraph, check whether there is an empty line before it in the output list.
Can it tell me how many paragraphs I wrote?
Yes. The paragraph counter shows the total paragraph count along with total words and the average words per paragraph.
Is this paragraph counter private?
Entirely. Your text is processed in the browser and is never uploaded or stored.
