About Us

Simple tools, honest engineering, zero tracking.

Who we are

eqseyu.com is a small collection of free online utilities built for people who just need things to work. No accounts, no paywalls, no pop ups. Open the page, get your answer, move on.

Why we built this

Writers run into the same problem all the time. You finish a draft and have no idea how many words it contains, whether it fits the limit for an essay, a Facebook post or a tweet, or how many characters it uses. Checking usually means hunting for a character counter and a word counter in one tab, then looking up each platform's limits in another. Papers make it worse. Different word counting websites return different numbers for the same text, some tools refuse to show a result until you sign in, and the counter inside Google Docs or Microsoft Word is easy to miss if you do not use those apps every day. So we built this site. Everything you need to count words lives in one place, free, accurate and without signup. We hope it helps students, teachers and anyone who writes check their word count quickly and confidently, submit text that meets the requirements, and make word counting easier.

Our principles

  • Privacy by default. Every tool processes your input locally in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to our servers. In fact, there is nothing to upload to.
  • Free means free. No trials, no locked features, no "pro" tiers. If a tool exists on this site, all of it works for everyone.
  • Fast and focused. Each tool does one job well, loads instantly and stays out of your way.

What's next

We're steadily adding new text and web utilities. If there's a tool you wish existed, tell us on the Contact page, requests from users shape the roadmap.
