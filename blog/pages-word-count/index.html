How Do You Get Word Count on Pages?

Step by step for Mac, iPad, iPhone and iCloud, plus fixes when the counter refuses to show up.

To get the word count in Apple Pages on a Mac, open your document, click View in the toolbar, and select Show Word Count. The counter appears at the bottom of the document. On iPad, open the view options and tap Show Word Count. On iPhone, tap More, then View Options, then Show Word Count.

You can use the counter to check your entire document or select a paragraph to count only that section. It also lets you switch between words, characters, paragraphs, and pages.

How to Show Word Count in Pages on Mac

Follow these steps to turn on word count in Pages for Mac:

  1. Open your document in Pages.
  2. Click the View button in the toolbar.
  3. Select Show Word Count.
  4. Find the counter near the bottom of the document window.

If the counter shows a different statistic, click the arrows beside it and choose Words. The available statistics include characters with spaces, characters without spaces, paragraphs, and pages.

You can drag the counter to reposition it. To remove it from view, open the View menu again and choose Hide Word Count. The menu label may change if you are displaying a different statistic, such as character count.

How to Show Word Count in Pages on iPad

To check the word count of a Pages document on your iPad:

  1. Open the document in the Pages app.
  2. Open the view options in the toolbar.
  3. Tap Show Word Count.
  4. Check the counter near the bottom of the screen.

Tap the counter to view more document statistics or select a different statistic to display. You can also drag the counter to a corner if it covers part of your text.

How to Show Word Count in Pages on iPhone

On an iPhone, the word-count setting is available through View Options:

  1. Open your document in Pages.
  2. Tap the More button at the top of the screen.
  3. Select View Options.
  4. Tap Show Word Count.

The counter appears near the bottom of the screen. Tap it to switch between available statistics, including word count and character count. You can drag it to a corner to move it out of the way.

How to Count Selected Text in Pages

To find the word count of a specific paragraph or section, turn on the word counter and then select the text you want to check. Pages displays the statistics for that selection instead of the whole document. This works on Mac, iPad, and iPhone.

For example, you can select only your introduction to check its length without counting the rest of your essay. Clear the selection when you want to return to the whole-document count.

How to Check Word Count in Pages for iCloud

You can also check word count when using Pages in a web browser:

  1. Open your document in Pages for iCloud.
  2. Make sure no text is selected if you want the total document count.
  3. Click View in the toolbar.
  4. Select Show Word Count.

Click the arrow beside the counter to see other statistics. If you select a passage, the counter shows the count for that selected text.

Why Is Word Count Not Showing in Pages?

If you cannot find the word count or the number does not look right, check these settings.

The counter is not enabled. Opening a document does not necessarily mean its counter is visible. Use the steps above for your device to turn on Show Word Count.

The counter is showing characters instead of words. Pages can display different statistics in the same counter. Click or tap the counter's controls and select Words to return to word count.

Only part of your document is being counted. If text is selected, the counter reflects that selection. Clear the selection to check the entire document again.

Is Word Count the Same as Page Count?

No. Word count measures the amount of text, while page count depends on how that text is formatted. Font, font size, line spacing, margins, headings, and paragraph length all affect how many words fit on a page.

For a rough estimate, a page of English text often contains around 500 words when single-spaced or 250 words when double-spaced. These are guidelines rather than exact conversions.

If your assignment or article brief specifies a word limit, use the word count shown in Pages rather than estimating from the number of pages. For a precise pages estimate from any word count, try our words to pages calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the word count in Pages on a Mac?
Click View in the toolbar and choose Show Word Count. The counter appears near the bottom of the document window.
Can Pages count characters, including spaces?
Yes. Pages can show character counts both with and without spaces. Open the counter's menu and choose the character-count option you need.
Can I count just one paragraph in Pages?
Yes. Enable word count, then select the paragraph. Pages shows the count for the selected text.
Do I need to copy my document into an online word counter?
No. To check a Pages document's word count, you can use the built-in counter on Mac, iPad, iPhone, or Pages for iCloud without copying the text into another tool. If you are working outside Pages, our free word counter gives you instant counts in the browser.
